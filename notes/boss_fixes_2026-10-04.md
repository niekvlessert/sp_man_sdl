# Boss fixes — 2026-10-04

## Fixed

- **Stage 9 core hit detection:** type `$79` now uses its visible 4×7 tile
  cells, like the other tile-based bosses. The former sprite proxy missed
  shots through the lower half of the visible core. The existing open/closed
  damage gate and ROM HP remain intact.
- **Terrain collisions in stages 3–9:** these stages previously fell through
  to stage 1's property table. In stage 9, normal player shots disappeared in
  the background before reaching the boss. The native port now decodes the
  common and stage/boss property tails from bank 10. `$8046`, `$818F` and
  `$81C2` traverse graphics records; `$8209` applies the property triples.
  Stage pointers are at `$8165` and `$8177`; the common group is `$83FD`.
  Main/boss tables are selected by the active boss/death actor. Existing
  dedicated stage 1 and stage 2 collision logic is retained.
- **Stage 9 destruction/ending:** surviving `$79` no longer receives an
  ordinary effect's 12-tick timeout. Its custom states 2/3 now run through
  the `$20`-tick final hold and the `$9717` ending latch. Previously the boss
  vanished before this completion path. Campaign completion is exposed in
  `PlaySession` and shown in the window title; credits are not implemented.
- **Music during destruction:** stages 7–9 now stop music at their custom
  death states (`$43`: 2+, `$78`: 6+, `$79`: 2+), matching the port's existing
  policy for the shared `$6A` destruction. Effects continue and music resumes
  when the next stage starts.
- **Stage 7 Warp Machine attack interval:** bank 5 `$8EE9–$8EF1` computes
  `next + $1A - difficulty`. The port used a constant `$18`, valid only for
  the previously traced `CA19=$02` loadout. It now uses the current difficulty.

## Verification

- Independently ran the original ROM in OpenMSX for every stage, captured all
  256 bytes at `DE00` at 15 seconds and during surviving boss encounters at
  330 seconds. All nine main tables and boss tables for stages 2–9 match the
  decoder byte for byte. Stage 1's final encounter uses another context and
  keeps its existing dedicated handling. FNV-1a capture hashes are checked
  in `stage59_test.cpp`.
- Stage 9 regression exercises **actual interactive firing** against the
  live boss, verifies HP loss, checks the lower core hit area, then tests
  fatal damage through the full custom destruction and ending latch.
- Stage 7 checks attack intervals at difficulties 1, 2 and 15; stages 7/8
  check music stop and resumption across the stage handoff.
- Build and stage 2, stage 3/4 baseline, stage 4/5 boss, stage 6/7/8/9 boss,
  stage 5–9 stream, combat, late combat, timeline and continuous-scroll
  checks passed.

These checks cover the identified collision, timing and completion bugs.
They do not establish full visual/audio parity for every boss or a complete
credits sequence. User layout changes and the existing stage 2 fidelity
report were preserved.

## Follow-up: lower Stage 2 parts and Stage 3/4 graphics

- Destroying a Stage 2 `$3C` machinery section (1 HP) converted it to `$6A`,
  the same effect type as boss death. The port's shared countdown stopped
  music, cleared vulnerable enemies and completed the stage regardless of
  the effect's origin. A native `$3F=$D3` marker now distinguishes machinery
  effects from real boss destruction. The core remains alive at its original
  32 HP; the lower section can still be destroyed.
- `$7A` spawns with metadata HP zero and receives its real 32 HP in `$A031`.
  Native collision could run during the intervening frames. Its vulnerability
  bit is now clear until the original initializer opens animation frame 4.
- Stage 3 `$3E/$3F`, Stage 4 `$14`, and Stage 5 `$77` were stamped into D988
  as well as drawn as SDL tile overlays. Coarse copies could use a different
  graphics page and leave fragments around the correct body. Both the main
  buffer and successor edge now exclude these SDL-owned tile actors.
- Stage 4's `$AFD4` draws five additional animated matrices after the static
  `$B03C` list: selectors `$26`, `$28`, optional `$2C`, and alternating 6/8
  or 7/9. These calls were missing entirely from the native compositor.
- Stage 3/4 use the original `$6C75` object raster anchors (`FF`/`F4`);
  their tile actors use graphics page `$0B/$01`. Stage 5's core uses
  `$2B/$05`. Boss palette preambles for stages 3–6 are decoded from the ROM
  boss groups rather than retaining the route palette. Stage 3's hardcoded
  palette override was removed, so every composition pass uses the same
  simulation palette.

`space-manbow-boss-visual-test` checks independent original-Z80 compositor
captures, not hashes generated by the native renderer. In OpenMSX, D988 was
cleared before `$7B65`; snapshots were taken at `$A655` for the Stage 3 shell
and at `$AE73` after Stage 4's static and animated parts. The native matrices
match every checked cell (32 visible columns × 24 rows); palette captures
also match. `tools/capture_boss_visuals.tcl` reproduces those fixtures with
`SM_BOSS_VISUAL_STAGE=2` or `3` and an existing `SM_BOSS_VISUAL_OUT` directory.
The follow-up on October 5 corrects the lower-section damage assumption:
player hits cannot destroy these sections at all. The provenance marker is
still used for their scripted destruction after the core dies.

Native SDL frames for stages 3/4 were inspected after these corrections.
The reference checks establish those captured poses, not every animation
phase of all nine bosses.
